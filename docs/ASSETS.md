# Asset provenance

All 12 dessert illustrations, the fallback and favicon are original SVG drawings authored for this project. No external product photos, downloaded fonts, templates or UI kits were used.

- Generator: scripts/create-illustrations.mjs. Run `node scripts/create-illustrations.mjs` to reproduce the 12 drawings and fallback.
- IDs match src/data/products.js.
- Local font stack: Segoe UI/Arial for controls, Georgia/Times New Roman for headings.
- Limitation: illustrations communicate product types; they are not photographs or promises of real goods.
- Reference screenshots are genuine browser captures for assignment investigation, not commercial product assets. They retain the reference site's branding solely as evidence.
- App screenshots are genuine captures of the production build served by Vite preview.
